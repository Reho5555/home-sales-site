How these numbers were made

  • Prices leave out the 40 sales under $10,000, which are not real market prices. Medians are used instead of averages, because a few sales run into the tens of millions.
  • Price per square foot also leaves out homes with no living area recorded or a living area of 0.
  • Days on market is the number of days from listing to going under contract. It is only counted for sales that have both dates (28,042 sales). Homes sold without a listing are left out, not counted as 0 days.
  • Sales counts every row for the city, including those left out of the price figures.
  • Every number is computed from data/college_town_home_sales.csv by scripts/build_summary.py, which writes data/site_summary.json.

Data: College-town home sales dataset, version 1.0 (2026). University of Illinois-Deloitte Foundation Center for Business Emerging Technologies. Data collected March 2026.
